FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Lutty: Your Sleepy Friend · Support: support@lutty.app

1. What is Lutty?

Lutty is a bedtime wind-down and sleep-habit companion for Android. At your scheduled bedtime, Lutty gently appears over your screen with a sleepy sloth friend, gradual warm dimming, and calming ambient sounds (such as waves, rain, or fire) to help you put your phone down and wind down for sleep.

2. Why does Lutty request "Display over other apps" and alarm permissions?

  • Display over other apps: Required so Lutty can show the bedtime overlay and warm screen dimming while you are using your phone at bedtime.
  • Alarms & reminders: Used to start your nightly wind-down ritual reliably on schedule.
  • Notifications: Used for pre-ritual reminders and foreground audio/overlay controls.
  • Physical activity (optional, for Smart Snooze): Used strictly on your device to detect if you are up and walking so Lutty can automatically pause your bedtime ritual until you settle down.

3. How does Smart Snooze work, and is my motion or sleep data uploaded?

No. Your sleep logs, bedtime schedules, ritual preferences, and Smart Snooze motion checks are processed and stored strictly locally on your device (with Android cloud backup disabled) and are never uploaded to Ambient Technologies OÜ's servers. You can erase all local data at any time by clearing Lutty's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

4. What is included in Lutty PLUS, and how does the 7-day free trial work?

Lutty PLUS unlocks full bedtime customization, all ambient soundscapes, Smart Snooze, and extended ritual features. Eligible new subscribers on the Yearly plan receive a 7-day free trial, which automatically converts into an annual subscription unless canceled before the trial ends. A Monthly plan is also available.

5. How do I switch plans, cancel my subscription, or request a refund?

All Lutty PLUS subscriptions are billed and managed exclusively through the Google Play Store:

  • Cancel or manage your subscription: Open the Google Play Store app → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → select Lutty (or tap Cancel Subscription inside Lutty's Settings).
  • Switch between Monthly and Yearly: You can switch plans inside Lutty's Settings (Change Plan). Your new plan price takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
  • Restore purchases: Tap Restore Purchases in Lutty's Settings while signed into the same Google Play account.
  • Refunds: Refunds are handled in accordance with Google Play's refund policy and applicable mandatory consumer laws.

6. What if the bedtime overlay doesn't appear or closes in the background?

Some Android manufacturers apply aggressive battery optimization that can delay scheduled alarms or stop background services. Make sure Display over other apps is enabled for Lutty and set Lutty's battery usage to Unrestricted in your phone's App Info settings.

7. Is Lutty a medical device?

No. Lutty is intended for general wellness and sleep-habit support only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice or diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any sleep disorder or medical condition.
